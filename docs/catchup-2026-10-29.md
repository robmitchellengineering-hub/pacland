# Catch-up — 2026-10-29: what we learned, and what it is for

Two sessions worked this repo today, on two nodes. This is the synthesis: the results with their numbers,
the failure pattern that produced them, and what any of it is good for.

## 1. What we learned

### The Riemann zeros — verified deeper, and one recorded reason corrected

| | |
|---|---|
| zeros computed from scratch below t = 20,000 | **22,491** against a smooth count of 22,491.4 |
| unfolded spacing | **1.00000** — the unfolding is exact |
| ratio statistic vs GUE control | **0.6133** vs 0.6137 ± 0.0018 — **0.2σ** |
| vs Poisson | 0.3810 — **133** control standard deviations |
| primes from the zeros (explicit formula) | ψ(x) to **±1 unit at 10⁵**; −27 at 10⁶; −57 at 10⁷ |

**The limit is a result**: the truncation error scales with √x, so each extra decade needs far more
zeros. It says exactly how many zeros a given x requires.

**And `0.5307` was the GOE value all along, not GUE's (#226).** The zeros are unitary; 0.5307 is the
real-symmetric asymptote. The 4σ scare was the **wrong ensemble**, not the sample size — the recorded
cause was wrong even though the physics verdict never was.

### The uniqueness theorem — confirmed, then scoped, then narrowed

- **one flux pair, full `sp(2m)`**: exactly **1** invariant for b3 = 2, 4, 6 — confirmed against the whole
  algebra, not a shear subgroup (#192);
- **two independent flux pairs**: **4** invariants — so *"the tadpole is the unique exact structure"* is a
  statement about **one pair**, and must say so;
- **higher degree (quartic/sextic)**: **never computed**. It still carries the label *"reasoned from the
  first fundamental theorem for Sp, not computed."*

### The swampland on T⁶ — tight, not approximate

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| distance-conjecture exponent | **α = 1.0000** at every volume |
| weak-gravity ratio (KK modes) | **z = 1.0000** at every volume |

Identities, not fits. A conjecture saturated in the controlled example is either exactly true — and the
toy shows the mechanism — or true only there. "α is of order one" is neither.

### The potential and the vacua — built, validated, and a question closed by the model

- `V = e^K(g^{ij̄}D_iW D_j̄W̄ − 3|W|²)` implemented; **4/4 validations**, including the no-scale identity
  (`3.0000000000`) and a constant-`W` cancellation (`0.000e+00`);
- a SUSY vacuum here is **AdS**: `V = −3e^K|W|²` to **1.9e-16**, with all-positive masses;
- **T⁶ has no vacua to take spacings of** — no Kähler moduli, so the potential is a runaway. The chaos
  question is **closed with a reason**, not left open.

### The h12 wall — truncation at degree 1, degree 2 fixes the series

| h12 | max move in cond(M), degrees 1→3 | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 2, 5 | 0.00% | geometry |
| 10 | **33.41%** | truncation |
| 25 | **88.39%** | truncation |

At h12 = 25, `d2 = d3` **exactly** at all five points while `d1` differs by up to 88%: **degree 1 is
unconverged, degree 2 is converged.** The sampler uses `maximum_degree = 0/1`.

**But the applied test narrowed it**: with the same budget, degree 2 did **not** find vacua that degree 0
could not (0 vs 0 SUSY at h12 = 5 and 10). **Degree 2 fixes the conditioning of the ISD matrix; it does
not by itself make the sampler produce vacua.** That is a smaller claim than the first one, and it is the
true one.

### GV integrality — a defect located precisely

| degree d | fitted n_d | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| (0,1), (1,0), (1,1) | −3.0004, −540.0009, 1080.009 | **integer** |
| (0,2), (2,0) | +5.6265, −607.48 | **NOT integer** |

**Every primitive class is integral; both non-primitive classes are not.** It recurs across disjoint point
sets (drift 1.9e-02 against a fit residual of 2.5e-07). That points at the **curve-class basis convention
for non-primitive classes**, not at the periods. Until settled: primitive-class `n_d` are usable, others
are not.

### The brane ring (F3) — one model is not a claim

| model | h11 | h12 | χ | χ/24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| quintic | 1 | 101 | −200 | **−25/3 — not an integer** |
| P1 × P3 lattice | 8 | 44 | −72 | **−3 — integer** |

Both satisfy `χ = 2(h11 − h12)` exactly. **The curvature-induced D3 charge is model-dependent**, so it
cannot be quoted as "the second exact invariant" without naming a model — and rung 1's "no rival" was true
on the quintic for the trivial reason that `h11 = 1`.

## 2. The failure pattern — one shape, five times

Every wrong result today was **the probe measuring something adjacent to what it claimed**:

| the probe | what it actually measured |
|---|---|
| a *real* finite-difference step on `K` | `Re τ`, when `K` depends only on `Im τ` — so every gradient was **0** and the no-scale identity read `0.0` instead of `3` |
| filtered transvections as "the symplectic group" | a **proper subgroup** — returned 1, 2, 3 invariants for b3 = 2, 4, 6: plausible, wrong, and it looked like a result |
| a `Li_3` fit with degrees beyond the y-window | **noise** — `e^{−2πd·y} ~ 1e-7`, so least squares reported "non-integer n_d" that were garbage |
| `|W|` at fixed moduli as a spectrum | a **lattice** — `|W|²` is an integer, so there are no fluctuations to report |
| the quintic as evidence about uniqueness | a **one-dimensional** divisor ring — no room for a rival to exist |

And three rules, each bought with a mistake:

1. **One sample is a draw.** B2 run 1 (one point per h12) said h12 = 25 was geometry at 0.47%; five points
   with instantons on said 88.39%. The correction cost one dispatch and a retraction.
2. **A check that cannot fail in the way you fear is not a check.** The old zeros test asserted
   `|control − 0.5307| > 0.05`, true under either ensemble, so it could never catch the mislabel.
3. **State the falsifier before the run.** It fired twice in the useful direction: on B1's first run (my
   test was wrong) and on F3's first candidate (χ/24 was refuted by its own run).

## 3. What we can do with it

### For the audio product (AirMatrix)

- **Weyl's law with the surface term** predicts a room's mode count to 0.02% — an oracle that needs no
  reference measurement;
- **two head-spaced positions condition a room's modal set**; ensembles say **4–7 usable parameters** at
  three microphones — quote the range, never the draw;
- **the Cramér–Rao bound says identifying 36 modal parameters to 10% needs ≈ −70 dB**; at a realistic
  −60 dB you get three, at −50 dB none. **So the product promise is margin and correction, not a modal
  parameter table.** What survives realistic noise is *combinations* — which is exactly what an EQ needs;
- **a fixed-Q graphic spills 9.29 dB of a 12 dB cut at Q = 4.32, against 0.18 dB at Q = 20** — parametric
  notches for modes, and the graphic for tilt;
- **never compare a finite measurement to an asymptotic constant** — the GoE/GUE mistake, in a room.

### For the research arm

- the **open claim** is computable: higher-degree invariants of the flux, or a theorem that none exist;
- **F2's certified periods** are the machinery that turns "degree 2 converges" into a **bound**;
- the **one item that can falsify C3** is discrete torsion in orbifolds — a label the orbit data cannot
  represent — and it is cheap to enumerate.

### For the estate

**The method is the product.** Every claim today carries an oracle class, a falsifier and a remedy; a
missing dependency is `NOT_DEMONSTRATED`, never `REFUSED`; a certificate that differs by one byte from a
fresh run fails. That discipline caught a mislabel inside a **VERIFIED** certificate today — which is the
strongest evidence for the discipline itself.
