# Mitchell's Principle of Constraint-First Unification

**Relations, Never Values**

> **Don't ask a complex system for its values — ask for its constraints, its count, and its
> deficit. Everything else is covariance, not knowledge.**

In its simplest form, one line of mathematics:

$$\mathbb{C}[F,H]^{\,Sp(b_3,\mathbb{Z})\times SL(2,\mathbb{Z})} \;=\; \mathbb{C}[N],\qquad N = F^{\top}J\,H$$

The ring of exact, duality-invariant quantities in the flux is generated by **one** invariant —
the tadpole. Everything in this document is a consequence of that one equation, stated honestly
and tiered: **proven** (symbolic / machine-checked), **measured** (node, with a control), or
**conjectured** (open). Never read one tier into another.

## The Principle

> **A theory of everything predicts relations, never values.** The exact content of a sector is
> exhausted by three types — **constraints** (consistency conditions), **labels** (topology), and
> **counting** (orbit-space dimensions). Everything else transforms by a **covariance table**. And
> there is **no selection** — not exact, not statistical, not cosmological.

One line: *a theory of everything is not one object — it is one **structure** (constraint + label
+ counting) repeated in every sector, wired together by the moduli.*

## Part 5 — The honest open gates

1. **The SM extension (conjectured).** "No UV completion derives the values correctly" is
   **conjectured**, not proven — the flux theorem needed a duality group + FFT, which the SM
   lacks. The surviving claim: no unique *value* is derived, only *relations*, and the relations
   that exist (SU(5) 5, SO(10) 12) are mostly wrong against data, leaving gauge unification (2).
2. **The dS uplift (the community's, ~0.70 trending-forbidden).** Every uplift mechanism now fails
   the 10D control test; the dS swampland is strongly supported but **not a theorem**. The decisive
   open question — *is the control problem generic?* — is string phenomenology's hardest open
   problem, honestly characterized, not ours to close.

## Falsifiers (each kills the Principle)

1. a moduli-independent, duality-invariant flux quantity that is **not** a function of `FᵀJH`;
2. an exact, derivable, *and numerically-correct* relation among the 19 beyond gauge unification (2);
3. a covariance law that **scatters** at higher h12;
4. a measured **non-uniform** `dN/d|W₀|²` near zero, or a proven dS no-go that empties the
   positive-Λ discretuum.
