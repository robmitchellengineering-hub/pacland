# Hypotheses from cross-referenced walls

Generated by `python3 scripts/hypotheses.py`. **Do not edit by hand.**

A wall alone is a place we got stuck. Paired with another it is a constraint, and constraints generate
hypotheses: *what must also be true for both to hold at once?*

**1 open · 3 supported · 2 refuted · 6 total**

Every hypothesis cites at least two walls **that exist in the wall register** — the link is checked by
`check_links()`, so a hypothesis cannot float free of the walls that motivated it.

**The claims-registry rule:** a hypothesis whose status is `supported` or `refuted` **must name a script that
exists in the repository**; `open` may say "no executable test". A decided hypothesis with no artefact is a
claim nobody can re-run. `check_artefacts()` enforces it and `scripts/hypotheses.py --check` fails on it.

## H-A — b3 is the only parameter, and the exponential is combinatorial

**status:** supported · **script:** `scripts/window_dependence.py`

**pairs these walls:**
- the measure wall: no principle selects a measure
- the enumeration objection: 'that's not the kind of problem it is at all'

**claim.** The measure dimension is exactly b3 (linear) while the content grows as 8.554^b3 (exponential) -- apparently incompatible until the closed form is read: P(x)^(b3/2) produces exponential growth from a FIXED polynomial. So the exponential is combinatorics, not a fundamental scale, and the only free parameter is b3.

**predicts.** no quantity grows in b3 faster than a polynomial-power count; every apparent exponential is a multinomial

**test.** DONE 2026-11-02: widen the window and the 'law' changes. 0<N<=1 counts go 20 -> 1,480 (per-unit-b3 8.60) at max_abs=1 but 52 -> 30,984 (24.41) at max_abs=2, matching (2*max_abs+1)^2 = 9 and 25. The exponential IS the window, so A = 8.554 is demoted to a window artefact; the hypothesis itself is SUPPORTED
