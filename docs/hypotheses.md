## H-A — b3 is the only parameter, and the exponential is combinatorial

**status:** open

**pairs these walls:**
- the measure wall: no principle selects a measure
- the enumeration objection: 'that's not the kind of problem it is at all'

**claim.** The measure dimension is exactly b3 (linear) while the content grows as 8.554^b3 (exponential) -- apparently incompatible until the closed form is read: P(x)^(b3/2) produces exponential growth from a FIXED polynomial. So the exponential is combinatorics, not a fundamental scale, and the only free parameter is b3.

**predicts.** no quantity grows in b3 faster than a polynomial-power count; every apparent exponential is a multinomial

**test.** look for any b3-scaling faster than a polynomial power; if none exists the exponential carries no independent content and a one-integer unification is viable

## H-B — measure-invariance IS observability

**status:** open

**pairs these walls:**
- the measure wall: no principle selects a measure
- the rate wall: yield falls ~100x from b3=6 to b3=12

**claim.** The measure is provably free and the sampler is budget-limited, so no sampler can ever resolve the measure. Therefore the only observables are the measure-invariant ones -- which makes the six INVARIANT entries, not the demoted ones, the entire observable content of the landscape.

**predicts.** the measure-invariant set and the computable-without-a-sampler set coincide

**test.** classify statistics by whether they are computable from exact counts alone; any statistic that is invariant yet needs a sampler, or vice versa, breaks the hypothesis
