# Resolving the uplift — is "small positive Λ via uplift" valid?

Status: **draft under test** (two adversarial literature passes in flight, 2026-10-23).
The question is the single most contested step in the CC story: the KKLT anti-D3 uplift.

## The question

The CC distribution arc ended on: the flux discretuum is *locally flat* near zero (measured /
pinned), but the "small positive Λ" requires the **uplift** `Λ = −3|W₀|²/(8σ³) + D/σ³`, and the
anti-D3 uplift is **contested**. This doc resolves whether that uplift — or a controlled
alternative — actually survives, which decides whether "small Λ via uplift" is (a) valid,
(b) broken, or (c) unsettled.

## Bottom line — the uplift is unresolved, and that is the honest answer

The anti-D3/KPV uplift is **contested, and the debate is still live** — there is **no controlled
10D de Sitter construction that the community accepts**, as of the literature catalogued so far:
the no-gos (Moritz–Retolaza–Westphal 1707.08678, Córdova–de Luca–Tomasiello 1812.04147,
Dall'Agata et al. 2203.12636, Hebecker–Schreyer–Venken 2208.02826) are real and unsettled, and the
refined dS swampland (1806.08362; 1807.05193; 1810.05506) would forbid positive Λ outright. So:

- **"small positive Λ via a controlled uplift" is (c) UNRESOLVED** — it is *conditional on a
  contested mechanism*, not a theorem;
- the **alternative uplifts** (D-term BKQ hep-th/0309187, LVS hep-th/0502058, F-term) each inherit
  or trade the same issues, and none is established as controlled;
- the **"anthropic selection"** on top is an **unresolved measure problem**, not a derivation.

**What this does to the CC story:** the "why this vacuum" arc now ends, honestly, with
*no selection anywhere we can reach* — no exact selector (proven), no flux statistical selection
(measured), and no *established* cosmological selection (the uplift that would supply small Λ is
contested). The small observed Λ is, at best, the anthropic tail of a locally-flat discretuum
*gated by a mechanism that is not yet under control*. That is the current, honest state — not a
peak, not a proof, an open gate.
