# The BMV "find it" numbers — where the experiment stands

**Status (2026):** the Bose–Marletto–Vedral / GIE experiment has **not yet** demonstrated
gravitational entanglement. It is an active frontier; the theory is being sharpened (the
entanglement-vs-decoherence "race" is exactly solvable, and entanglement survives with a nonzero
floor — decoherence does *not* doom the test). No published mass-entanglement result exists yet.

## The entangling phase, exact

For two masses `m₁, m₂`, each in a spatial superposition of width `Δx`, center distance `d`, held
time `t`, the BMV entangling phase is

```
φ = 2 G m₁ m₂ t Δx₁ Δx₂ / (ħ d³)
```

## The numbers

| configuration | φ |
|---|---|
| **BMV proposal** (m = 10⁻¹⁴ kg, d = 100 µm, Δx = 10 µm, t = 1 s) | **0.013 rad** |
| same, t = 1000 s | 12.7 rad |
| m = 10⁻¹² kg, d = 100 µm, Δx = 10 µm, t = 1 s | 127 rad |
| m = 10⁻¹⁴ kg, d = 10 µm, Δx = 1 µm, t = 1 s | 0.13 rad |

**Hierarchy:** BMV mass / Planck mass = 4.6 × 10⁻⁷ (the tabletop masses are ~10⁶·³ below Planck).

## What this means

- The 10¹⁵ deficit is an **energy** gap; the BMV test sidesteps it by measuring a **phase** at low
  energy, where gravity is weak. The weakness shows up as a *small phase* (φ ~ 10⁻²), not an
  unreachable energy.
- φ ≈ 0.013 rad is **measurable**: it needs ~10⁴ statistics to beat shot noise, or one of the
  three dials (longer `t`, larger `m`, smaller `d`) to reach φ ~ 1.
- So the experiment is **at the edge of feasibility, not beyond it** — and it is the *direct* test of
  our kernel-checked no-go: classical gravity (LOCC) cannot produce this phase-as-entanglement, so
  observing it forces the coupling to be quantum.

## The concrete "where to look"

The three dials and their cost:

1. **Longer hold time** (t → 10³ s) — free, but needs isolation from seismic/acoustic noise.
2. **Larger mass** (m → 10⁻¹² kg) — the hard one: putting a microgram in a delocalized superposition.
3. **Smaller distance** (d → 10 µm) — tightens the Casimir/electrostatic control.

The sweet spot is a combination of (1) and (3) — longer time at small distance — avoiding the
microgram-mass hard problem. That is the regime the experiment will "find it" in.
