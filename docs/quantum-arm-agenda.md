# The quantum arm: the biggest questions we can actually reach

Rob, 2026-10-28: *"what we are looking for are answers to the biggest questions in the universe."*

The arm is the estate's research arm, and it is what produced the AirMatrix direction. So this is not a
list of topics; it is a list of **questions with a feasibility judgement attached**, and one exact
answer already in hand.

## 1. The first exact answer: in the toy, the swampland conjectures are TIGHT, not approximate

On the exact T⁶ oracle the two sharpest conjectures are **identities**, computed (not fitted):

| quantity | value | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| distance-conjecture exponent | **α = 1.0000** for every volume | the tower mass falls exactly as `e^{-Δφ}`; the bound is saturated, not slack |
| weak-gravity ratio for KK modes | **z = 1.0000** for every volume | the WGC is saturated exactly |

That is a real statement about the toy, and it is the shape of answer this arm should produce: *not* "the
conjecture is plausible", but "here is the exact coefficient, and here is whether the bound is tight".

**Why it matters:** a conjecture that is saturated in the controlled example is either exactly true (and
the toy shows the mechanism) or exactly true only there (and the toy shows where the mechanism is the
whole story). Both readings are useful; "α is of order one" is not.

## 3. The question I would attack next, and why

**Question 1: is the landscape chaotic?**

It is the only one on the list where (a) the answer is unknown to us, (b) our existing, *verified*
machinery applies without modification, and (c) the answer would be a fact rather than a preference.

The machinery is already validated on a spectrum with a known answer: the Riemann zeros. Computing a
mass spectrum for an ensemble of flux vacua and asking whether its levels repel (GOE) or cluster
(Poisson) is the same statistic, the same control, the same falsifier. If the landscape's masses are
Poisson, the landscape is integrable — its vacua do not interact — and the "10^500 vacua" picture is a
list, not a structure. If they repel, the vacua are coupled by the flux lattice and the landscape has a
rigidity that no one has described.

**Either answer is a result.** That is the test of a good question.

## 4. What this arm cannot do, said plainly

- It cannot solve the cosmological constant problem. It can measure how much of the "explanation" is a
  statement about a measure.
- It cannot compute a real Calabi–Yau spectrum — the oracle is T⁶, and the gap to a real CY is the
  largest single piece of missing machinery in the arm.
- It cannot produce an experimental prediction. Nothing here is testable in a laboratory; what is
  testable is internal consistency and exactness, and the discipline says so everywhere it grades.

## 5. The rule the arm works under

Every claim carries its oracle class and its falsifier, or it is class "no oracle" and is recorded as
such. The arm's output is **exact structure and measured statistics**, never plausibility.
