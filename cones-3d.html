Kähler cones in 3D

What the flux sampler needs before it can place a modulus. Ray and hyperplane counts are measured from the installed jaxvacua; the angular arrangement is schematic.
Why this page exists. At h12 = 3 the sampler is constructed with no cone data at all — _hyperplanes, _rays and _extremal_rays are all None — so every sampling path that needs rays fails. A gap in the model table, not a licence and not physics.

Measured, not invented: the counts come from s._rays.shape on pacland-rog, 2026-10-31.
