Climate — the verification, in 3D

Same data as the 2D figures, drawn as three scenes you can rotate: drag to turn, scroll to zoom, hover a dot for the model. Each scene says what to look for.

1 · The leak

Axes are the three conservation ledgers, each in its own units. A model in a world with no forcing should sit at the origin. Red dots are the ones that drift away — 14 of 19; green ones hold.

2 · The trust gate

Climate sensitivity against heat trapping, with conservation drift as the third axis — the 19 models that have a conservation test, since a model with no drift data has not been tested. The dashed box is where a model has to land: plausible sensitivity is not enough, it has to survive all three axes.

3 · The fix

Drift correction subtracts each model's own leak: every red dot returns to the origin. That is what a repaired model looks like — and it is why the repair is computable instead of a research programme.
