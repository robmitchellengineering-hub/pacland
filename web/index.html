String theory is ~10500 vacua and no experiment that tells most of them apart. "Unified string theory" fuses two questions — does some vacuum match us? and why that one? — and the field has no verification layer for either. PACLAND is that layer, built with Valiant's probably-approximately-correct learning: a certificate that bounds a candidate's error even when the verifier is itself approximate, a measured prior, a negative result, and a map of the computable.

Mitchell's Principle of Constraint-First Unification: don't ask a complex system for its values — ask for its constraints, its count, and its deficit. Everything else is covariance, not knowledge.

In one line: ℂ[F,H]Sp×SL(2,ℤ) = ℂ[N], N = FᵀJH — the invariant ring has a single generator (the constraint); the free content is the orbit-space dimension (19); everything else transforms covariantly (Mitchell's Laws of Duality Covariance). The unification, stated correctly

Three things are true at once — and they are not the same claim. The difference between them is the whole result.

1 · Disproven — the formula

The naive dream — "derive the 19 Standard-Model numbers from a Calabi–Yau" — is disproven for the flux sector. No exact, polynomial, duality-invariant formula can do it. That is a theorem (machine-checked), not an opinion.

2 · Replaced — the characterization

Unification still exists, reframed: constraint + label + counting + covariance. Proven for the flux; conjectured for the Standard Model (it has no duality group + FFT, so the proof does not transfer — stated as a gap, not claimed).

3 · Open — statistical selection

The no-go is on exact invariant selectors only. Statistical selection — the measure over vacua, "why this one" — is not ruled out. We killed the equation; we did not kill the statistics.

The headline consequence. The only exact, polynomial, duality-invariant selector in the flux landscape is the tadpole. So no exact invariant function can single out a vacuum — but this is a no-go on exact invariant selectors, not on search in general: statistical, moduli-dependent, or non-polynomial selection is not ruled out. Over the integers the tadpole is not even a complete invariant — the content d = gcd(F,H) (d²|N) labels finitely many orbits per tadpole.

The falsifiable edge, stated plainly: no exact flux observable beyond the tadpole can ever be found. A single counterexample — one moduli-independent, duality-invariant flux quantity that is not a function of FᵀJH — falsifies the whole tower. That is what makes it science, not slogan.

Mitchell's Laws of Duality Covariance. The theorem says what's invariant; the laws say what's covariant — measured on real supersymmetric vacua at h12 = 2 and 5, each gated by a control: |W_S|/|W| = 1, arg(W_S/W) = −arg τ, g_s → |τ|² g_s under S-duality, and the moduli inert at degree 0. Duality is realised as a covariance, never an anomaly — "one theory described twice" is literal, and the laws are h12-independent.

The Five Pillars. The no-fingerprint result rests on five independent families of theorems, each forcing a piece of the conclusion:

pillarforces

The four walls are what you can't do; the five pillars are what makes the claim stand. Three pillars sharpen the result: the vacuum count is bounded, not fixed by topology; the constraint is a K-theory vanishing (total charge = 0 in K(X)); the vacuum space is a symplectic quotient (Marsden–Weinstein). The Standard-Model frontier — constraint-first, one sector up

The Complete Exact Structure Theorem proved the flux sector's only exact structure is its constraint plus its label. The same classification extends one sector up to the Standard Model — the m = 0 frontier the theorem named as its open question.

typeflux sector (proven)Standard Model
constraint — "what the theory forbids"D3 tadpole N ≤ L (the unique invariant)anomaly cancellation, charge quantization
label — "which geometry you picked"h¹¹, h²¹, c₂, κgauge group SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1); N_gen = 3 = a topological index (|χ|/2 in the heterotic class)
parameter — unpredicableW₀, g_s, couplings, masses, Λthe ~19 free numbers (masses, couplings, mixing angles, θ_QCD)

The checkable piece: scripts/validate_sm_anomaly.py verifies, exactly (rational arithmetic, zero floating point), that the SM fermion content cancels all five gauge/gravitational anomalies per generation — [SU(3)]³ = [SU(3)]²U(1) = [SU(2)]²U(1) = [U(1)]³ = [grav]²U(1) = 0 (5/5). The hypercharges (1/6, 2/3, −1/3, −1/2, −1) are forced by these vanishing conditions — required for consistency, not predicted.

The gauge-sector uniqueness theorem (scripts/validate_sm_hypercharge_uniqueness.py, 4/4, exact — the SM analog of Mitchell's Uniqueness): given the SM matter content (gauge reps + chirality), the anomaly conditions determine the hypercharge assignment uniquely, up to overall normalization and the u↔d swap. The three linear conditions ([grav]²U(1), [SU(2)]²U(1), [SU(3)]²U(1)) leave a 2-dimensional family (Y_L = −3Y_Q, Y_e = −6Y_Q, Y_d = 2Y_Q − Y_u); the cubic [U(1)]³ collapses it — 18·Y_Q·(2Y_Q + Y_u)(4Y_Q − Y_u) = 0 ⟹ Y_u = 4Y_Q (the SM) or Y_u = −2Y_Q (the u↔d swap). The gauge constraint is unique, exactly as the flux constraint (the tadpole) is unique.

The invariant ring — 19 = orbit-space dimension. scripts/validate_sm_invariant_ring.py makes the FFT analog exact: the 19 parameters are the orbit-space dimension of the flavor group (quark sector 36 − 27 + 1 = 10, lepton 18 − 18 + 3 = 3, plus gauge 3 + Higgs 2 + θ 1). They are the independent flavor invariants — nothing relates them within the SM, by a theorem. And scripts/validate_gut_parameter_count.py shows minimal SU(5) leaves ~19 (not 14): gauge unification saves 2 but the Higgs sector grows ~2 — a shuffle, not a reduction.

The generalization. In every sector, the exact structure is constraint + label; everything else is a free parameter. Flux sector: proven. SM sector: anomaly cancellation (5/5), hypercharge uniqueness (4/4), the invariant ring (19 = orbit-space) — all exact.

The refined claim (from two research fan-outs). The first fan-out split "unpredicable" and found the honest form: a ToE predicts relations (invariants), never values — GUTs and string geometry relate the parameters but never derive their absolute values. The second fan-out split "free parameter" and found the floor is a structure, not a number: 0 continuous, 1 discrete (the tadpole), 19 relations. See docs/sm-conjecture-notes.md and docs/sm-floor-notes.md.

The falsifiable test: derive any one of the SM's ~19 free parameters from first principles and the constraint-first generalization is falsified. The Rule of Three — no result from one test

No "structured vs. random" claim is made from a single measurement. Every such claim needs three independent confirmations that agree: single-coordinate mutual information, the joint ridge-quadratic test, and a permutation null. The tadpole (r = 1.0 on all three) is the positive control proving the tests work. This rule is what caught and corrected our own W₀ over-reach — "zero-information" was a single-coordinate artifact; the joint test gives r = 0.62.

Also confirmed from data: the no-scale theorem — SUSY flux vacua sit at V = 0 to machine precision (10⁻³¹), before Kähler-moduli stabilization. The honest wall chain: SUSY (86.8%) × stability (4.6%) = 87.2% of critical points ruled out.

The Mitchell Verification Deficit — real, and working

The deficit is the count m of observables that are simultaneously measured, computable, and validated. The certificate's oracle term is √(ln 1/δ / 2m), so at m = 0 the certificate is vacuous (infinite radius), and every validated observable shrinks it. It is not a slogan — it is a number we have moved, by building and validating oracles.

The 0–2000 m ocean alone under-closes the energy budget (residual 0.15 W/m²); the full budget — ocean + atmosphere + land + cryosphere — closes it (0.01 W/m²). The budget is only an invariant when all reservoirs are counted.

energy-budget closure

The CMIP6 conservation drift (real data, three ledgers). A conserving model must close three independent ledgers — energy, mass, salt. Measured: 12 of 19 leak energy (two — CNRM-ESM2-1 at 1.485 W/m², CNRM-CM6-1 at 1.46 — at ~1.9× the Earth's energy imbalance), 6 of 19 leak mass (ACCESS-ESM1-5 at −1.03 mm/yr, ~30% of sea-level rise), but only 2 of 19 leak salt. Salt — the purely-ocean ledger — is conserved orders of magnitude better, so the leak lives in the atmosphere-ocean coupling, not the ocean model. Crossed with the field's two other quality axes — climate sensitivity (ECS) and effective forcing (ERF2x) — only 2 of 19 survive all three axes — the GISS family.
