The MERA causal cone is a discrete hyperbolic space

The MERA tensor network embeds a binary tree in the Poincaré disk — the hyperbolic plane. Level k carries 2^k sites on a circle of hyperbolic radius k: exponential volume, logarithmic depth. This is the picture behind "entanglement = geometry" (Swingle's MERA = AdS), now a machine-checked theorem (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24045807).

What the disk shows. The centre is the IR (one coarse-grained state); the rim is the UV (the physical boundary). A local operator near the rim touches only O(log N) tensors — the logarithmic causal cone — which is why the geometry is hyperbolic, not flat. Exponential volume, logarithmic depth, and the area law are the three finite facts that make it a discretization of the hyperbolic plane.
