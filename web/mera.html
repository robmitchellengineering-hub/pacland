Geometric entanglement — the engine that drives SEXTANT

The MERA causal cone is a discrete hyperbolic ball: 2k sites live on a sphere of hyperbolic radius k, from one coarse-grained state at the centre (gold IR) to 28 sites at the boundary (teal UV).

Entanglement is encoded in the geometry: cut the ball anywhere, and the entanglement across the cut is the number of bonds the cut slices — the area. That area law is exactly what SEXTANT's entanglement-area falsifier runs.
