SEXTANT

The universe has been answering yes or no all along — SEXTANT is how you finally hear it. A quantum-enabled yes/no hypothesis engine: bare-metal algebraic proofs from first principles, for discovery and synthesis. It makes quantum physics deterministic in use — the methodical, step-by-step unravelling of the universe's deepest mysteries. Ask it a claim, and it doesn't guess — it runs the simulation, checks the machine-checked theorem, and hands you the verdict:

FORCED — the universe had no choice. A theorem, machine-checked in Lean. This is what must be true.

FREE — the universe shrugged. A dial it never explained — the free parameter you get to turn.

IMPOSSIBLE — the universe said no. Linearity itself forbids it. Stop chasing it — the door was never there.

Why this is discovery, not measurement: finding what's FORCED is the discovery — the theorems the universe cannot escape. Building on them is the synthesis — because once you know what must be true, what can't be true, and exactly where the free dials sit, you stop guessing and start constructing.

What it does not do, said plainly: it does not compute values — it classifies them (relations, never values). It is not a faster quantum computer — it rides the same simulators everyone uses. It does not invent physics — it certifies what is already provable, and says honestly where the proof stops.

What it does

Every claim below gets one of the three verdicts, and every verdict is earned twice over: a falsifier that actually runs (a simulator tries to break the claim, and the claim has to survive it), and a Lean theorem that the kernel checked (the proof, not just the number). Tap any claim to read both — the academic proof and a one-page plain-speak explainer. Then download the node and run the falsifiers yourself: python -m pacland.sextant_node — no cloud, no account, no trust required.

What it makes possible

Once you know what is FORCED (the theorems the universe cannot escape), what is IMPOSSIBLE (the doors that were never there), and exactly where the FREE dials sit, the job of physics changes shape. You stop guessing at values and start constructing from constraints — the difference between fitting a curve and knowing the curve is forced. That is the constraint-first Theory of Everything, made executable: relations, never values. The three open frontiers — Švarc–Milnor, the even-n Cayley, the Gromov–Hausdorff convergence — are where the community extends it.
