SEXTANT

Ask it a claim about the quantum world, and it doesn't guess — it runs the simulation, checks the machine-checked theorem, and hands you the verdict:

FORCED — the universe had no choice. A theorem, machine-checked in Lean.

FREE — the universe shrugged. A label, not a law; you can measure it, you can't derive it.

IMPOSSIBLE — the universe said no. A no-go: linearity itself forbids it.

What it does not do, said plainly: it does not compute values — it classifies them (relations, never values). It is not a faster quantum computer — it rides the same simulators everyone uses. It does not invent physics — it certifies what is already provable, and says honestly where the proof stops.
