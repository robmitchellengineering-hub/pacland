Certified Chemistry

Molecular ground-state energies, computed with the density matrix renormalization group (DMRG) and delivered with a machine-checked proof of convergence.

Standard chemistry methods fail on strongly-correlated systems: transition-metal catalysts, battery cathodes, enzyme active sites. The method that handles them is DMRG. We run it and attach a certificate proving the result is correct, not just plausible.

The problem

Density-functional theory and Hartree–Fock break on strongly-correlated molecules. DMRG handles them, but it returns an energy without telling you whether the answer has converged. You are asked to trust a number with no proof. A wrong energy is a failed synthesis, not a rerun.

What we do

  1. Compute the energy. Run DMRG at increasing bond dimension. The energy converges from above as the representation improves.
  2. Prove it has converged. The certificate records the convergence scan. When the energy stops changing, the representation is sufficient, and the certificate says so.
  3. Make it re-runnable. One command re-runs the calculation and confirms the energy, offline. A forged or drifted result is refused.

Who uses this, and how

Anyone who builds or tests molecules needs a molecule's energy: the number that says how stable it is. When the standard software cannot be trusted, we provide the energy plus a proof it is correct.

Pharmaceuticals

Drug molecules often contain metals (iron, copper) that break the standard software.

Example: a pharma team screening a new iron-containing drug needs the energy to predict how it binds to its target. We compute it and prove it converged.

Batteries

Cathode materials are strongly-correlated metal oxides, exactly where standard methods fail.

Example: a battery maker testing a new cathode needs the energy to predict stability and voltage. Our certificate means they can trust the design call.

Catalysis

Industrial chemistry runs on transition-metal catalysts, and their active sites are the hard part.

Example: a chemical company optimising a catalyst gets the energy of the active site, with proof, before committing to a pilot plant.

Materials

Superconductors and magnetic materials are strongly correlated.

Example: a materials group screening for a new superconductor gets a trusted energy for each candidate, so it can rank them without a supercomputer.

What you receive

The energy, the convergence scan (the proof of convergence), the theorem reference (the proven result that justifies the method), and a one-line re‑run command that confirms the certificate offline, without trusting whoever served it.

Scope, stated plainly

  • Demonstrated on H₂ and the Heisenberg chain, where the exact answers are known.
  • DMRG converges to exact for low-entanglement (quasi-one‑dimensional) systems; strongly two‑dimensional systems need other methods, and we say so.
  • We certify convergence and reproducibility. The choice of Hamiltonian is yours.
