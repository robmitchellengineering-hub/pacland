Quantum gravity: the referee, and where it stops

Thirteen conditions, six frameworks, and one rule — no verdict without a reader. Every number here comes from code in src/pacland/energy/ and can be re-run.

1 · Why a referee and not another theory

Graviton perturbation theory is non-renormalisable: [G_N] = −2, so a new counterterm is needed at every loop order. The consequence is a degeneracy, not a mystery — infinitely many ultraviolet completions reduce to Einstein gravity below the Planck scale, so every candidate framework matches every current observation automatically. Below the Planck scale they are general relativity.

Discrimination can therefore only come from an observable where a completion must deviate, or from a condition a framework must satisfy and does not. The field checks the second one framework at a time, inside each community. This is the joint check.

A blocker is CONTESTED, NOT-ESTABLISHED or NO-PREDICTION — a finding about the framework. NOT-RESEARCHED never counts: "we have not looked" is a gap here, not a property of the framework. The claim that all six are blocked can fail, and a test shows how — delete the C6 entries for cdt and gft and it goes false.

3 · The condition that decides everything: C6

C6 asks for an account of the vacuum energy, ρ_Λ/M_Pl⁴ = 1.1e-123. It is the cheapest place to test whether a framework can survive all thirteen, so the whole row was researched.

frameworkposition
causal-setspredicts a magnitude — an "everpresent Λ", right order, constrained by CMB isotropy (not excluded)
asymptotic-safetyclaims to solve it by predicting Λ = 0; the observed Λ > 0 is then a tension
strings-adscftlandscape + anthropic selection: an order of magnitude, not a value
lqg-spinfoam, gft, cdtno account found — the arXiv search that came back empty is recorded as the source
No framework can survive all thirteen conditions. Three engage and are contested, three make no claim, none is a pass. And C6 cannot kill either, by construction: failing to account for Λ is UNKNOWN, not KILLED, or all six would die by fiat on a question nobody has solved.

5 · Carrier A, rung 1 — the fixed point, against its own closed form

The functional renormalisation group, Einstein–Hilbert truncation, Litim's optimised cutoff (arXiv:hep-th/0312114). python3 -m pacland.energy.frg:

lambda*        = 0.2500000000
g* (physical)  = 1.1780972451      closed form 1.1780972451

theta          = 1.666678 +/- 4.307624 i   (paper: (5 +/- i sqrt(167))/3)

Three independent published results reproduced. The exponents are complex, and that is the published answer — the paper gives θ± = (5 ± i√167)/3 and records that the pair stays complex for 2.56 < d < 21.4. A solver that refuses complex exponents fails on the known answer; ours did, before the paper's LaTeX was read.

6 · The cubic, and negative gravity — how a misread formula nearly shipped

The beta functions were first extracted from the PDF, where the superscripts are mangled: the text is ambiguous between d³g and d·3·g. The "obvious" reading drops a +2 from P2. That produces a cubic where the paper says the condition is quadratic, and it drives g negative while still converging.

Why the general object is a quartic, and the paper's quadratic

At a fixed point P2 = 0 forces g* = (λ−½)²/(4d). Substituting into P1 gives

P1 = (d+2)·(2λ−1)²·Q(λ) / (4d),      Q(λ) = A λ² + B λ + C
A = 4d(d−4)(d+1)      B = −4d(d²−d−4)      C = d(d²−d−4)

So P1 is a quartic that factors as (2λ−1)² × quadratic. Dividing by g* ∝ (2λ−1)² — which is what "insert into P1/g* = 0" means — leaves the quadratic the paper describes, with real roots for d ≥ (1+√17)/2 = 2.5616.

The cubic appears only at d = 4, because the quadratic's leading coefficient A = 4d(d−4)(d+1) vanishes there. The equation degenerates to linear, giving λ* = ¼ — which is exactly the 0/0 limit of the paper's closed form.

Negative gravity is an artefact, not a branch

Dropping the +2 forces g = λ(λ−1)/4, which is negative for 0 < λ < 1. At d = 4 that "solves" at

λ = 0.000000   g = −0.000000
λ = 0.233756   g = −0.044778
λ = 0.891244   g = −0.024232

g < 0 means G < 0 — a wrong-sign kinetic term, a ghost. But these are not even fixed points of the true flow: the correct P2 evaluates to +2 at every one of them, so β_g ≠ 0. And the correct equation gives g* = (λ−½)²/4d ≥ 0 for every λ — the Einstein–Hilbert truncation has no negative-g fixed point at all.

The wrong formula produced a mathematically consistent, physically impossible fixed point. That is why the source of a formula is part of the result, and why the misreading is now a mutation test rather than a sentence in a report.

7 · Rung 2 — one rung reached, the rest refused

The polynomial f(R) truncation (Machado & Saueressig, arXiv:0712.0445), flow equation, ansatz and result tables read from the LaTeX source. python3 -m pacland.energy.frg_poly:

n=1  u* = [+0.00523, −0.02021]                     paper: [+0.00523, −0.02020]   MATCH
n=2  u* = [+0.00374, −0.02111, +0.00337]           paper: [+0.00333, −0.01250, +0.00149]   mismatch
n=3  u* = [+0.00098, −0.01093, +0.00172, +0.00015] paper: [+0.00518, −0.01960, +0.00070, −0.01040]   mismatch

n = 1 reproduces the published fixed point exactly. n ≥ 2 does not, and the paper says why: its trace decomposition carries a single-mode contribution D₁(d,0)·(1/P_k)·∂_tR_k evaluated at −D² = Λ₁(d,0), which is not a heat-kernel term and so does not appear in the dimensionless flow equation. The authors' own footnote attributes the difference to exactly that term.

So rung 2 claims no ladder. It claims the machinery is correct and that the published n ≥ 2 ladder cannot be reproduced from the published flow equation alone. A test asserts the mismatches are reported, so they cannot be tuned into agreement later without deleting the test — because a truncation ladder that silently omits a term looks exactly like a converging ladder.

8 · What this is not

  • Not a theory of everything — the repo has already shown no connecting equation derives the 19 Standard-Model parameters.
  • Not a claim to have solved quantum gravity. It claims to decide between candidates, and to say honestly when it cannot.
  • Not a kill. Nothing here is promoted to KILLED or SURVIVES without a named source.
