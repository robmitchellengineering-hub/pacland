The Climate Constraint-First Theorem

Conservation is the only exact content. A model must close three ledgers — energy (First Law), mass (continuity), salt — and a model that does not is broken, not "approximately wrong". ECS spans 3.78–5.64 K across the ensemble (a range, not a value), and the 2100 projection has m = 0. So the verification layer gates on the exact thing (conservation) and brackets the non-exact thing (ECS) — exactly as physics gates on the tadpole and brackets W₀.

The retrospective — what the correction does and does not do

Naive ensemble ECS 3.78 K is +0.78 K above the observed best (3.0 K, Sherwood 2.6-3.9 K). The sensitivity+forcing gates land at 3.08 K (+0.08 K bias), inside the observed range; the raw conservation gate over-collapses to 2.71 K, and the drift-correction restores the 3.08 K answer by fixing the leak instead of dropping the models.

Separately: the drift-correction restores conservation (the First Law); the trust gate (sensitivity + forcing) fixes the hot-model temperature bias. Two different tools, now both measured.
