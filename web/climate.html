Climate, drawn from the data

Four pictures, each produced by the same code that verifies the claim. Nothing here is hand-drawn: regenerate with scripts/build_climate_charts.py.

1 · Heat leak, model by model

What a model says it took in, minus what the ocean actually gained. The orange line is the measured Earth energy imbalance, 0.77 W/m² — the signal these models exist to predict. 2 of 19 leak faster than the real signal itself.

2 · The three conservation ledgers

A model in a world with no forcing should be stationary. 10 of 19 fail on energy, 2 on ocean mass, 0 on salt. Salt is the ledger that only involves the ocean — it is conserved far better than the two that cross the air–sea boundary, which is how we know where the bug is.

3 · How well each predictor actually did

Mean absolute error on a real-time hindcast, lower is better. The trend — the warming so far, continued in a straight line — beats every emulator built from a published climate sensitivity. That is the whole finding of the null: the simplest guess is hard to beat.

4 · The energy budget, and what closes it

Every reservoir the Earth has, in W/m². The budget closes to 0.01 W/m² with all of them — and misses by 0.15 W/m² if only the upper ocean is counted. That gap is the argument for measuring the deep ocean.
